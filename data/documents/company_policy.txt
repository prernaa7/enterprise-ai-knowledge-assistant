Company Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave

Employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per year.

Annual leave should be requested in advance through the appropriate leave request process and must be approved by the employee's manager before the leave is taken.

Employees are encouraged to plan their annual leave in advance to ensure adequate team coverage and smooth business operations.

2. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per year.

If an employee is unable to work due to illness, they should inform their manager as soon as possible.

For extended periods of illness, the employee may be required to provide appropriate medical documentation in accordance with company requirements.

3. Work From Home

Employees may work from home with prior approval from their manager.

Employees should communicate their planned work-from-home schedule to their manager in advance.

While working from home, employees are expected to remain available during normal working hours and complete their assigned responsibilities.

4. Leave Carry Forward

Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the following year, subject to company policy and manager approval.

The amount of leave that can be carried forward may be subject to applicable company rules and limits.

Employees should check their available leave balance before planning extended leave.

5. Public Holidays

Employees are entitled to company-designated public holidays in addition to their annual leave entitlement.

The list of public holidays may vary depending on the employee's location and applicable company holiday calendar.

Employees should refer to the official company holiday calendar for the applicable dates.

6. Leave Approval

All planned leave should be submitted through the designated company leave-management process.

Leave is subject to manager approval and business requirements.

Submitting a leave request does not automatically mean that the leave has been approved.

Employees should wait for confirmation before making plans that depend on leave approval.

7. Emergency Leave

In case of an emergency, employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Emergency leave may be reviewed and approved based on the circumstances and applicable company policies.

8. Extended Leave

Employees who require an extended period of leave should discuss their situation with their manager and the appropriate HR representative.

Additional documentation or approval may be required depending on the type and duration of the leave.

9. Leave Balance

Employees are responsible for keeping track of their leave balance.

Employees should regularly check the company's leave-management system to verify their available annual leave and sick leave.

Any discrepancies should be reported to the appropriate HR or administrative team.

10. Policy Updates

The company may update its leave policies from time to time based on business requirements, applicable regulations, or changes to company procedures.

Employees should refer to the latest official company policy documents for current rules and requirements.
